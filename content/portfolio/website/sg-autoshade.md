SG Autoshade are [vehicle body repair specialists](https://www.sgautoshade.co.uk) based
at Blinkbonny Farm in Gorebridge, with over 15 years' experience in accident repairs,
resprays and restorations for customers across Edinburgh and the Lothians.

They needed a website that would let local drivers find them, see the full range of
work on offer, from car body and accident repair to alloy wheel, dent and scratch
repair, and get in touch for a free quote. Both private and insurance work are
handled, so the site needed to make that clear up front.

#### A Bold, Fast Static Site

The design leans into the business's own branding, with a dark, full-bleed hero and
the red, orange and yellow from the SG Autoshade logo carried through the headings
and calls to action. "Get A Quote" sits in the header on every page so a customer is
never more than a click away from making an enquiry.

I built the site with [Nuxt JS](https://nuxt.com/) as a statically generated site,
styled with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) and hosted on
[Netlify](https://netlify.com/). Images are served in modern formats (WebP and AVIF)
through Nuxt Image, and the site includes structured data so search engines
understand the business, its location and its contact details. It's also set up as a
Progressive Web App, so customers can add it to their phone's home screen.
