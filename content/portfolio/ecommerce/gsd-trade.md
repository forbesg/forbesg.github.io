## A Trade-Only Ecommerce Platform

Following on from the [Granite Setts Direct](/portfolio/ecommerce/granite-setts-direct/)
retail site, I built [GSD Trade](https://www.gsdtrade.co.uk), a dedicated B2B platform
for supplying wholesale granite setts, kerbstones and paving to trade customers such as
landscapers, architects, builders merchants, civil engineering companies and local
authorities.

Unlike the retail site, GSD Trade is not open to the general public. Customers create a
trade account and log in to shop, so the whole experience is built around the needs of
professional buyers placing bulk orders rather than one-off retail purchases.

## How It's Built

The site is built with [Nuxt JS](https://nuxt.com/), server-side rendering pages for
speed and search visibility. Product, cart, account and order functionality come from
the [ElasticPath](https://www.elasticpath.com/) headless commerce API, with payments
handled by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/).

Page content is managed in [DatoCMS](https://www.datocms.com/), so the team can update the site without needing
a developer. The product catalogue is organised by stone type, finish and colour, with
site-wide search to help trade buyers find exactly what they need quickly. The site is
hosted on [Netlify](https://netlify.com/).
