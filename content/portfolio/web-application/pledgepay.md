## Clear Agreements, Certain Payments

[PledgePay](https://www.pledgepay.net) tackles a problem every tradesperson, freelancer
and customer will recognise: payment disputes that start with vague agreements and money
changing hands at the wrong time. Customers worry about paying too early; professionals
worry about being paid at all.

PledgePay puts clarity in place before the work starts:

1. **Agree the job:** the scope, price and expectations are written down.
2. **Commit the payment:** the client secures the funds before work begins.
3. **Do the work:** the professional gets on with the job knowing the money is there.
4. **Confirm and release:** payment is released when both sides are satisfied.

If something does go wrong, both sides have a calm, structured way to raise a concern,
linked to what was originally agreed rather than to who remembers what.

## How It's Built

The platform is made up of two parts: a marketing site at pledgepay.net, and the
PledgePay app itself, a Progressive Web App that customers and professionals can install
on their phone and use like a native app.

Both are built with [Nuxt JS](https://nuxt.com/). The app uses
[Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) for sign-in and data, with
[Stripe](https://stripe.com/) handling the payments. PledgePay is currently in beta.
