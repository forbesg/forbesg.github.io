## Edinburgh Based Website Design & Development

As a website developer I love to solve problems. Talking in plain English, I help my clients find the right online solution for them and their customers. By focusing on my clients' success I can ensure that their customers come back again and again. Although based in Edinburgh, I regularly undertake website development projects remotely.

Below you will find some of the services I provide, but I am always happy to discuss any new ideas or ventures.

If you need a simple brochure website to broadcast your business to the world, I can provide a static website in no time at all. If you are not looking to update your website regularly this is a perfect option and, as with all static websites, **hosting is free**.

## Content Management System Website Development

### Need to create and update your website?

If you expect your site to grow and you will be updating it regularly, you will need a content management system, which lets you get stuck in and create and update your site yourself.

I provide web development services using traditional PHP/MySQL content management systems such as WordPress and Drupal, but I prefer a more modern approach using **headless CMSs**, giving you the speed and security of a static site with the dynamic nature of a traditional CMS.

[Content Management System Websites](/services/cms-websites/)

There are multiple ways to get your online shop up and running, and I can always find a suitable solution for any budget.
