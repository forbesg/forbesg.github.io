## Website Redesign, Speed & SEO in Edinburgh
### Make the website you already have work harder

A website that takes an age to load, looks dated on a phone or never shows up on Google is costing you customers. You don't always need to start again: often the right fixes to the site you have will make a big difference.

I start by measuring. Google's Lighthouse and Core Web Vitals show exactly what's slowing your pages down, and Search Console shows how people find you, or don't. From there I'll tell you in plain English what's worth fixing, what it will cost, and what you can expect to change.

Sometimes the honest answer is that the platform is the problem. If your site is weighed down by plugins and updates, I can move it to a fast [static or headless website](/services/cms-websites/) that you can still edit yourself, and redirect every old address so you keep the rankings you've built up.

I work with businesses across Edinburgh, Leith and the Lothians, and further afield remotely.
